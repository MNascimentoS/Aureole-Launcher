Features

Everything Aureole does, from A to W.

A

  • Alphabet on the edge

    Slide along the letters on the edge and the drawer jumps right to the one you pick, highlighted.

B

  • Built for one hand

    Menus and settings within thumb reach, with a left-handed mode.

C

  • Cards and glass

    Tune the corners, blur and opacity of the cards.

  • Clocks

    Styles like time only, date on top or a daily greeting, with your font, format and colors.

  • Containers

    Group apps and folders in vertical or horizontal blocks, with optional background and labels.

F

  • Favorites

    Build favorites lists and choose which apps stay on top.

  • Folders

    Folders with a name and an icon picked from a library sorted by category.

  • Fonts

    Fonts like Montserrat, Orbitron and Special Elite for the home screen and the drawer.

  • Free layout

    Drag to move and pull the edge to resize, with side panels, in portrait or landscape.

G

  • Guided welcome

    In under a minute, a short walkthrough teaches the gestures and sets up your screen. No account, no login.

I

  • In-app release notes

    Every update explains what changed.

P

  • Palettes

    Dozens of color palettes, previewed the moment you tap.

Q

  • Quick button

    Opens an app or shortcut on tap, long press or swipe.

  • Quick search

    Find apps and their widgets by name, with the search bar near your thumb.

T

  • Themed icons

    Icons follow your theme colors.

U

  • Undo

    Removed something by mistake? One tap brings it back.

W

  • Widget stacks

    Put several widgets in one spot and swipe between them.

What stays and what leaves

No account, login or backend server. Only one thing leaves your device, and only when the app crashes.

Stays on your device

  • App list
  • Folders and favorites
  • Clocks
  • Widgets
  • Wallpaper
  • Settings

Leaves the device

A crash report, sent through Firebase Crashlytics when the app crashes. No name, contacts, location or app list.
