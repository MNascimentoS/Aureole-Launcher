Features

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with its own Design System.

  • Folders and favorites

    Group apps and keep the ones you use most within reach.

  • Panels

    Organize your home screen into pages the way you like.

  • Widgets

    Add widgets from other apps right to your home screen.

  • Wallpaper

    Pick any photo with the Android photo picker, no gallery permission needed.

  • Quick search

    Find and open any app in a few taps.

  • No ads

    No account, no login and no analytics tools.

Your data stays on your device

  • No account, login or backend server
  • Your app list never leaves the device
  • Only technical crash reports (Crashlytics)
